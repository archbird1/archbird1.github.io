ABOUT THE BIBLE VERSIONS

HITHERTO contains no Bible text. You read in your own Bible, and the app keeps count.

Verses are counted using each translation's published verse numbering, so 100% means every verse in the version you chose. Editions of the same translation can differ by a verse or two, so treat the totals as a close guide.

Translation names and abbreviations (KJV, NKJV, NASB, NIV, NIrV, ESV, NLT, CSB, NRSV) are used only to identify those versions. They belong to their respective owners. HITHERTO is not affiliated with or endorsed by any Bible publisher, church or ministry.
