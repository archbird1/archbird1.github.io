PRIVACY POLICY

HITHERTO · last updated November 12, 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

HITHERTO is a Bible reading tracker, made by Lohm Lab.

HITHERTO does not collect anything about you. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you put into the app stays on your device.

WHAT THE APP KEEPS ON YOUR DEVICE

  • Which verses you have marked as read
  • When you last marked something, so the app can take you back to it
  • Your settings: Bible version, colors, font and corners

All of it is saved in the app's own private storage on your device. Lohm Lab cannot see it and never receives it.

WHAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE

Nothing, unless you send it yourself. The app does not send or receive data over the internet.

  • Android device backup. If you have turned on your phone's backup to your Google account, Android may include this app's data in that backup. That copy is handled by Google under your Google account, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

PERMISSIONS

  • None. HITHERTO asks your phone for no permissions at all.

DELETING YOUR DATA

You can delete things inside the app at any time. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything it saved.

Because nothing is sent to Lohm Lab, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

CHILDREN

HITHERTO is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

CHANGES

If a later version of HITHERTO changes how any of this works, this policy will be updated first and the date on it will change.
