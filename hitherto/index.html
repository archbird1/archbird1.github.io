HITHERTO

Keep track of the ground you've covered.

HITHERTO is a Bible reading tracker. Read in your own Bible; HITHERTO keeps count of the verses you've read and gives you a visual way to see your progress through Scripture.

AndroidIn developmentFree · No ads
⬡A record of the reading.

About HITHERTO

Made for the way you use it.

HITHERTO is built around a simple distinction: it does not provide Scripture text. You read from the Bible you already use, then mark your progress in HITHERTO. Its name comes from 1 Samuel 7:12: “Hitherto hath the LORD helped us.”

01

A whole-Bible picture

A pyramid of 66 hexagons represents the books of the Bible. Each book fills as you mark your reading.

02

Mark at your own pace

Mark a chapter as read, or open it to select individual verses. Jump back to your last marked place.

03

Progress with context

See whole-Bible, Old Testament, and New Testament totals, alongside shelves that fill as you read.

Inside the app

Thoughtful details. Practical tools.

Finding and marking

  • Look up a reference or book by typing a name, abbreviation, or passage.
  • Tap a chapter to mark it; press and hold to open verse selection.
  • Tap or drag across verses to mark the passages you've read.
  • Use JUMP TO to return to your last marked place.

Bible versions

  • KJV and NKJV are grouped together and enabled by default.
  • Also supports NASB, NIV and NIrV, ESV, NLT, CSB, and NRSV.
  • Progress follows the verse numbering of the selected version.
  • Changing versions does not erase existing marks.
HITHERTO does not contain or display Bible text. It tracks reading progress only. Translation names identify supported verse-numbering systems; the app does not reproduce copyrighted translation text.

A Lohm Lab product

Simple by intention.

Free to use, with no ads, no accounts, and no permissions. It works offline, and your reading progress stays on your device.
