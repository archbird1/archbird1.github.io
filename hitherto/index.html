HITHERTO

Bible Tracker

A Bible reading tracker. You read in your own Bible. HITHERTO counts every verse you’ve read and shows how far you’ve come.

The name

“Hitherto hath the LORD helped us.”

1 Samuel 7:12, King James Version. The verse is on the app’s opening screen.

What it does

The dashboard

  • Everything on one screen, with nothing to scroll.
  • A pyramid of 66 hexagons, one for each book, with Genesis at the bottom left and Revelation on top. Each one fills from the bottom as you read that book.
  • Tap the pyramid to switch between the whole Bible, the Old Testament and the New Testament.
  • Two shelves of book spines, Old and New Testament, that fill as you read.
  • Look up: type “mat”, “heb 11” or “ps 23:4” and it opens as far as you typed.
  • Jump to: go straight back to the last place you marked.

Marking what you’ve read

  • Tap a chapter to mark it read.
  • Hold a chapter to open its verses, then tap or drag across the ones you’ve read.
  • Reading is dated the day you mark it. Read it earlier? Change the date to the exact day, just the month, or just the year, whatever you remember.

Advanced dashboard

  • Your progress over time, for the whole Bible or each testament. Touch the chart to see any day.
  • The days you read, the books you have started and finished, and how far you are through each part of the Bible.

Readers and read-throughs

  • A profile with your name, picture and milestones. Every book, section and testament you finish, added up across every time through.
  • Start a new read-through when you begin again. The old one stays, and you can switch back any time.
  • More than one person can keep their reading on the same phone.

Bible versions

  • Six choices: KJV and NKJV (the default), NASB, NIV and NIrV, ESV, NLT, and CSB and NRSV. Versions that number their verses the same way share a choice.
  • Progress is counted against your version’s own verse numbering, so 100% means every verse in the Bible you hold.
  • Switching versions never loses a mark.

What it isn’t

HITHERTO contains no Bible text. It is a tracker, not a reader.

Version names appear only so the verse count matches the Bible in your hands.
