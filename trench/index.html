TRENCH

Notes Board

A notes app built around a board. Pin notes where you can see them, and file the rest in folders as deep as you like.

In development

At a glance

Status
In development. Not yet on Google Play.
Platform
Android
Price
Free. No ads and no in-app purchases.
Account
None. There is nothing to sign up for.
Internet
Never used. The app has no permission to go online.
Permissions
Camera, only when you take a photo for an image note. Screen lock or fingerprint, only for “Forgot PIN?”.
Screen
Portrait and landscape
Your data
Stays on your phone. You can export everything to a backup file and import it again.

What it does

Notes

  • Four kinds: Text, Image, Canvas for drawing, and Counter.
  • Tags, colors and comments on anything.

The board

  • A bulletin board of pinned cards for notes and folders.
  • The add button floats. Drag it wherever suits your thumb, and choose one of seven shapes for it.
  • Filter the board by tag or by color.

Folders

  • Folders inside folders, as deep as you like. Drag to reorder, or drop one into another.
  • Folder categories with around 50 icons.
  • Family tree: a top-down chart of everything inside a folder. Pinch to zoom.

Finding and keeping

  • Sonar: a search drawer that looks through titles, note text, comments and tags.
  • Locks: put any note or folder behind a PIN. You choose whether one entry unlocks just that item or everything, and how long it stays unlocked.
  • Recovery: deleted items wait 16 days before they are gone for good.
  • 6 color palettes and 6 fonts.

What it isn’t

The lock is not encryption. It hides an item behind a PIN inside the app, which is enough to stop a glance over your shoulder. It is not a vault, and it is no place for passwords.

TRENCH does not sync between devices. To move your notes, export a backup and import it on the other phone.
