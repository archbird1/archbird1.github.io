The workshop

How a thing gets made here.

one idea at a time

Behind the studio is one man’s workshop. It’s where I take an idea and find out whether it can become something useful.

The ones that survive get the slow treatment. I design them, try them on my own phone, write down everything that feels wrong, and go around again. CLK-01 went around that loop until it came out simpler than it went in.

  1. Conceive

    It starts with a question worth answering. How simple can a chess clock be and still handle everything a real game throws at it?

  2. Experiment

    I build the smallest version and live with it. HITHERTO was a Bible reader before it became what it is now: a tracker with no text in it at all.

  3. Develop

    Then the stubborn parts. HITHERTO counts your reading against six different verse numberings, each one checked against the published text.

  4. Refine

    Round after round of notes until nothing snags. All three apps share one settings layout, so learning one teaches you the other two.
