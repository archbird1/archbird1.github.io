Privacy Policy

CLK-01. Last updated November 24, 2026.

THE SHORT VERSION

CLK-01 stores its data on your phone, and the app itself never sends it anywhere. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include it in your device backup. This policy covers CLK-01, a chess clock for Android made by Lohm Lab (lohm.dev).

WHAT CLK-01 KEEPS

CLK-01 saves four things, in its own private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read them.

  • Your settings: palette, corners, font, vibration, sound, keep screen awake and the low-time warning.
  • Your custom time controls: each one's label, starting times, and increment, delay or Bronstein bonus.
  • Your last-used time control, so the clock opens on it next time.
  • The game in progress: both clocks, the move counts and the time control, so a game can be offered back if your phone closes the app. If that happens while a clock is running, the recovered times are from the last move, pause or resume, not the exact moment the app closed. The saved game is removed when the game ends or you reset it.

If your phone's own Android backup is turned on, Android may include these settings in your backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your account; Lohm Lab never sees it.

WHAT CLK-01 DOESN'T DO

CLK-01 doesn't use the internet. The app sends nothing to Lohm Lab or anyone else, and Lohm Lab never receives your settings or games.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No personal information collected: no name, contacts, location, photos or files.
  • No analytics, crash reports or tracking.
  • No ads and no advertising IDs.
  • Nothing shared with or sold to anyone.

The sounds are built into the app. Nothing is downloaded.

PERMISSIONS

CLK-01 asks Android for one permission: vibration. It is used only for the optional buzz on each move, at low time and when time runs out. You can turn it off in CLK-01's settings.

DELETING YOUR DATA

Uninstalling CLK-01, or clearing its storage in Android's settings (Settings → Apps → CLK-01 → Storage → Clear storage), removes everything it keeps on your phone. A single custom time control can be deleted from the time-control list in the app. Any copy already in your Android backup is managed separately, through your Google account.

CHILDREN

CLK-01 isn't directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If CLK-01 ever starts keeping or sending anything new, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date, at lohm.dev.
