CLK-01

A chess clock with a little more history.

CLK-01 is made for over-the-board chess: set the time, choose the players, and put one phone between the two sides. It keeps the clock simple while giving your games somewhere to go afterward.

About CLK-01

Made for the way you use it.

CLK-01 brings a dedicated chess clock together with player profiles and the records that grow around regular games. It is designed for casual play at home, with friends, or among a small group.

01

A clock that gets out of the way

Open straight into the clock. Choose players and a time control, then start the game with a tap.

02

Players and rivalries

Create player profiles, track records overall or by color, and see head-to-head results between rivals.

03

More than one game

Keep a full game history, award a House Champion title, and organize casual tournaments.

Inside the app

Thoughtful details. Practical tools.

Time controls

  • Built-in time controls from 5 minutes to 1.5 hours.
  • Increment presets including 3|2, 5|3, 10|5, 15|10, and 90|30.
  • Create custom controls, including uneven starting times for handicaps.

Records and events

  • Player cards with photos or chess-piece placeholders.
  • House Champion title changes hands when the current champion is beaten.
  • Swiss, single-elimination, and round-robin tournament formats.
  • Tournament Champion title; players can display up to three titles.
CLK-01 is intended for casual, over-the-board play. It is not a certified clock for rated or official competition and is not affiliated with FIDE, US Chess, Chess.com, or Lichess.

A Lohm Lab product

Simple by intention.

Free to use, with no ads and no accounts. The app works offline; player data and game records stay on your device. Vibration is the only permission.
