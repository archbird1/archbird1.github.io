CLK-01

Chess Clock

A chess clock for over-the-board games at home. Lay one phone on its side between two players. It keeps the time, then keeps the record.

What it does

The clock

  • Opens straight onto the clock. Tap a side to choose that player, pick a time control and press Start.
  • Ten built-in time controls, from 5 minutes a side to 90 minutes with a 30-second increment. An increment is extra time added to your clock after each move.
  • Custom time controls, including uneven times for a handicap game.
  • The clocks wait until the dark side taps, which starts light’s clock.
  • Resign, draw offers, pause and a move counter.
  • Flag rules as played over the board: running out of time is a loss, or a draw if your opponent could not possibly checkmate.

Players and records

  • Player profiles with a photo or a chess-piece placeholder.
  • Each player’s record overall, as light and as dark.
  • Rivalries: the head-to-head record between any two players.
  • A full history of every game played.

Titles and tournaments

  • House Champion, a belt that changes hands whenever the champion is beaten.
  • Tournaments in three formats: Swiss, single elimination and round robin.
  • A Tournament Champion title for each winner. Players choose up to three titles to show on their card.

What it isn’t

CLK-01 is for casual play. It is not a certified clock for rated or official games, and its tournaments are made for the kitchen table.

It is not affiliated with FIDE, US Chess, Chess.com or Lichess.
