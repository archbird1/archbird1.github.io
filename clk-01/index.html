CLK-01

Chess Clock

A chess clock for the board in front of you. Lay one phone on its side between two players, and it keeps the time. Nothing more.

Your data

What it does

The clock

  • Opens straight onto the clock. Pick a time control, and the clocks wait until dark taps, which starts light’s clock.
  • Ten built-in time controls, from 1 minute a side to 30. A control like 3|2 means 3 minutes each, plus 2 seconds after every move.
  • Custom time controls with your own label, including uneven times for a handicap game.
  • Swap colors or swap times before the game starts.

Three kinds of bonus time

  • Increment: seconds added to your clock after each move.
  • Delay: a few free seconds at the start of each turn before your clock starts running down.
  • Bronstein: after each move, you get back the time you used, up to the bonus.

During a game

  • Pause at any time. While paused, either player’s time can be adjusted, from a button along that player’s own edge of the screen.
  • Undo a mis-tap. The time since the tap is charged to the player who tapped, so undo never hands out free thinking time.
  • A move counter, a low-time warning, and optional sounds and vibration.
  • Keeps the screen awake during a game.
  • If your phone closes the app mid-game, the game is offered back when you return.

Its look

  • Four color palettes: Viridian, Graphite, Amethyst and Ink-well. Corners and font can be changed too.

What it isn’t

CLK-01 is a clock and only a clock. It keeps no player profiles and no game history.

It is for casual play, not a certified clock for rated or official games. Check with your organizer before relying on it for anything official.

It is not affiliated with FIDE, US Chess, Chess.com or Lichess.
